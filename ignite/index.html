Why it matters

One community. One light.

From the university students who find a Shabbat table at our home, to the toddlers singing their first Modeh Ani, to the family who needs a hot meal this week — Chabad of Riverside is where our community comes together. For 72 hours, every gift is doubled, and every dollar goes straight into the people who walk through our doors.

Ignite a Child

Jewish Academy, Kids Empire camp and holiday programs that give our children joy and pride in who they are.

Ignite a Student

Chabad at UCR: Shabbat dinners, learning and a home away from home for hundreds of students.

Ignite a Teen

CTeen nights, trips and leadership that help our teenagers grow into confident young Jews.

Ignite a Woman

Women's Circle gatherings, classes and friendships that turn a city into a community.

Ignite a Home

Our new community mikvah, mezuzahs and the traditions that build strong Jewish homes.

Ignite a Family

Social services, holiday meals and a phone that's always answered when a family is going through a hard time.
